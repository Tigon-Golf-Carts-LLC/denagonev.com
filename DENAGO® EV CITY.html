What Is a City Cart Golf Cart?

A city cart golf cart is a street-legal electric vehicle designed for short-distance travel around neighborhoods, master-planned communities, downtowns, and resorts. Classified as a low-speed vehicle (LSV), a city cart golf cart typically tops out around 25 MPH, carries four to six passengers, and is equipped with the safety features required for road use — headlights, turn signals, mirrors, seat belts, a windshield, and a horn. Unlike a basic golf cart that's limited to the course, a city golf cart can legally travel on most public roads posted at 35 MPH or less, making it a practical, fuel-free alternative to a second car for errands, commuting, and getting around town.

Benefits of a City Golf Cart — FAQ

What is a city cart golf cart?

A city cart golf cart is a street-legal, low-speed electric vehicle (LSV) built for neighborhood and around-town driving. It typically seats four to six, reaches about 25 MPH, and includes road-legal equipment like lights, mirrors, seat belts, and a windshield, so it can travel on most roads posted at 35 MPH or less.

Is the Denago City street-legal?

Yes. The Denago EV City is a fully street-legal low-speed vehicle (LSV) equipped with headlights, turn signals, mirrors, seat belts, a windshield, and a horn. It is legal to drive on most public roads posted at 35 MPH or less, wherever state and local laws permit.

How far can a city golf cart go on one charge?

It depends on the model. Many city golf carts travel 25 to 40 miles per charge, but the Denago EV City leads its class with up to 80 miles of range from its 51.2V lithium battery — enough for days of neighborhood driving between charges.
