Denago Nomad XL Price & Configurations

The Denago Nomad XL price starts at $9,995 MSRP for the 2025/2026 model year. That base price already includes the 5 kW AC motor, LiFePO4 lithium battery, all-terrain tires, a 2.5-inch lift, rear camera, and the Denago sound system — no costly add-ons required to get a fully street-legal, four-passenger cart.
