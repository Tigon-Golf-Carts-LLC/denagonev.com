Why Choose the Denago XL6?

The Denago XL6 is built for anyone who needs to move a full group without giving up comfort or capability. With six seats, all-terrain tires, a 3-inch lift, and a 5 kW AC motor, the Denago XL6 handles jobs a four-seat cart can't. For families, it means everyone rides together — kids, grandparents, and gear — on a single street-legal electric vehicle that's safe and easy to drive around the neighborhood. For resorts, hotels, and golf communities, the Denago XL6 is an efficient shuttle for guests and staff, with a 40-mile range that keeps it running through busy days and a premium cabin that reflects well on your property. For master-planned communities, HOAs, and large properties, it's a practical, zero-emission alternative to a second car for errands, events, and getting around. Add Apple CarPlay, a rear camera, and a comfortable six-passenger layout, and the Denago XL6 becomes the most versatile pick in the Rover family for group transportation.
