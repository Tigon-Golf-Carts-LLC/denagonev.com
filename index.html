Why Buy Your Denago Golf Cart from Tigon?

Tigon Golf Carts is a factory-authorized Denago dealer, and this page is your complete hub for every Denago golf cart model sold today. Denago EV builds some of the most refined street-legal electric carts on the market — purpose-built low-speed vehicles (LSVs) that blend golf-cart agility with real automotive features like LED lighting, touchscreen displays, rear cameras, and lithium battery power. Whether you are commuting across a master-planned community, cruising the neighborhood, or heading off-road on the trail, there is a Denago cart engineered for the job. Below you'll find the full Denago lineup — the City, Nomad, Nomad XL, Rover XL, Rover XL6, and Rover XXL — with starting prices, headline specs, and direct links to compare each model side by side.

Why buy your Denago golf cart from Tigon? We stock the entire range, so you can compare models instead of settling for whatever a single-model dealer happens to have. From the value-focused 4-seat Nomad at $9,995 to the flagship 6-seat City at $16,500, every Denago cart is fully street legal where local law permits and ready for years of dependable, zero-emission driving.
