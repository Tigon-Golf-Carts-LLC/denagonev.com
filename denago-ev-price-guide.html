Denago EV Price Guide – Full Lineup MSRP & Dealer Pricing

Wondering about the Denago EV price for each model? This guide lays out transparent 2025/2026 starting MSRP for the entire Denago EV lineup, plus financing, value comparisons, and answers to the most common pricing questions — all in one place from Tigon Golf Carts, your factory-authorized Denago dealer.

How Much Does a Denago EV Cost?

Denago EV prices for the 2025/2026 model year range from $9,995 to $16,500 MSRP. The four-passenger Nomad is the most affordable Denago EV at $9,995, while the six-passenger flagship City sits at the top of the lineup at $16,500. Most models land in the $10,000–$13,000 range, giving buyers a street-legal, lithium-powered electric cart at a competitive price point.

  • Entry-level ($9,995): The Nomad and Nomad XL deliver street-legal, four-passenger lithium power at the lowest Denago EV price.

Is a Denago EV a Good Value?

Compared with other street-legal, lithium-powered low-speed vehicles (LSVs) — many of which run well into the teens and twenties once you add comparable features — Denago EV pricing is competitive for what you get. Every Denago EV includes a lithium battery (with a 5-year battery warranty), a 2-year parts warranty, and a lifetime chassis warranty, plus modern touches like LED lighting, touchscreen displays, and rear cameras on higher trims. Factor in Tigon's nationwide shipping, financing, and 90-day return policy, and the total cost of ownership compares favorably to both big-box golf carts and pricier boutique EVs.
