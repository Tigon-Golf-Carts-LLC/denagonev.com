How to Choose the Right Denago Golf Cart

Choosing the right Denago golf cart comes down to four questions: how many people you need to carry, where you'll drive, how far you travel between charges, and your budget. Here's how the Denago lineup breaks down so you can match a cart to the way you'll actually use it.

  • Seating (4 vs 6): The Nomad, Nomad XL, and Rover XL seat four passengers, while the City, Rover XL6, and Rover XXL seat six — ideal for larger families or carrying guests.
  • Terrain: For neighborhood streets and smooth community roads, the City and Nomad shine. If you want trails and off-road capability, the Rover series adds all-terrain tires and rugged styling.
  • Range: Most models deliver about 40 miles per charge. The City leads the lineup with up to 80 miles, making it the best choice for longer days and larger communities.
  • Budget: Pricing runs from $9,995 for the entry-level Nomad to $16,500 for the fully equipped City, so there's a Denago golf cart for most budgets.

Denago Golf Cart FAQ

What is a Denago golf cart?

A Denago golf cart is a street-legal electric low-speed vehicle (LSV) built by Denago EV. Each model blends golf-cart maneuverability with automotive features like LED lighting, touchscreen displays, lithium batteries, and seating for four to six passengers, making it ideal for neighborhood, community, and short-distance travel.

How much does a Denago golf cart cost?

Denago golf cart pricing at Tigon starts at $9,995 for the four-passenger Nomad and ranges up to $16,500 for the six-passenger flagship City. The Rover XL starts at $10,995, while the six-seat Rover XL6 and Rover XXL start at $12,995. Financing options are available on every model.

Are Denago golf carts street legal?

Yes. Every Denago golf cart is built as a street-legal low-speed vehicle (LSV) with DOT-style features such as headlights, turn signals, mirrors, seat belts, and a windshield. They are legal on most roads posted at 35 MPH or less, where permitted by state and local law.
